About Us

About Sunshine Homes

Based right here in Auckland, Sunshine Homes Limited has been delivering outstanding residential construction across the region for over a decade. We know the local landscape, councils, and communities inside out.

As a Master Builder accredited company, we uphold the highest industry standards on every project — big or small. Our team of skilled tradespeople bring precision, care, and professionalism to everything they build.

With 10+ years of experience and 50+ homes under our belt, we've built a reputation on trust, transparency, and results that exceed expectations every single time.
