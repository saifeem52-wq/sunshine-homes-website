FEATURED PROJECT 01 25-Unit Residential Development

48–50 Atkinson Avenue

📍 Ōtāhuhu, Auckland

Architectural plans for a substantial residential development comprising 25 modern, multi-level homes in Ōtāhuhu, Auckland. The development features contemporary terrace-style housing, incorporating ground-level garaging, upper-level residential accommodation and a combination of contrasting exterior cladding materials. The site layout brings together multiple dwelling configurations within a coordinated residential development.

FEATURED PROJECT 02 Three Attached Residential Homes

28 Arthur Road

📍 Hillpark / Manurewa, Auckland

Architectural plans for three attached, two-storey family homes at 28 Arthur Road, Manurewa (proposed Lots 1–3). Each residence is designed with three bedrooms, ground-floor living spaces and integral garaging. The homes feature contemporary external cladding, pitched roofing and a coordinated architectural style designed to provide practical family accommodation.

3 Planned Dwellings (Lots 1–3) 3 Bedrooms Per Dwelling Two-Storey Construction Integral Garages

FEATURED PROJECT 03 10-Bedroom Boarding House

28 Pine Street

📍 Balmoral / Mount Eden, Auckland

Architectural plans for a purpose-designed, two-storey boarding house at 28 Pine Street, Balmoral, Auckland. The development is designed to accommodate ten bedrooms, including accessible accommodation on the ground floor. Shared facilities include a communal kitchen, dining and living spaces, alongside a manager's office. The proposed building provides approximately 354.9 m² of total floor area.

10 Bedrooms Two Storeys (~354.9 m²) Accessible Ground-Floor Unit Communal Facilities & Office

FEATURED PROJECT 04 Large Lifestyle Residence

572 Redoubt Road

📍 Flat Bush, Auckland

Architectural plans for a substantial lifestyle residence at 572 Redoubt Road, Flat Bush. The design includes seven bedrooms, spacious living and dining areas, outdoor entertaining spaces, a swimming pool, spa and proposed lift. The residence is arranged across multiple wings on a sloping site, with approximately 863.35 m² of combined ground-floor and first-floor area.

7 Bedrooms ~863.35 m² Floor Area Swimming Pool & Spa Proposed Lift & Outdoor Decks

FEATURED PROJECT 05 14-Bedroom Visitor Accommodation

12 Gifford Road

📍 Papatoetoe, Auckland

Architectural plans for a two-storey visitor accommodation development at 12 Gifford Road, Papatoetoe. The building is designed to provide fourteen bedrooms, including two accessible bedrooms on the ground floor. Shared facilities include a reception area, communal lounge, kitchen and dining space. The exterior design combines brick cladding at ground level with contrasting dark vertical cladding above.

14 Bedrooms Two Storeys 2 Accessible Bedrooms Communal Dining & Reception
