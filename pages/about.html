Our Story

A personal approach to every build.

Sunshine Homes Limited was founded in 2014 on a straightforward principle: great building starts with listening carefully to how people want to live. Based in Ōtāhuhu, Auckland, we work closely with homeowners, landowners, and investors across Greater Auckland.

Whether we are constructing a brand-new family residence from the ground up, reimagining an existing home through renovation, or helping prepare a property for its next chapter, we treat every project with the same care and attention to detail.

Our team combines local Auckland council and site experience with practical project coordination, transparent communication, and skilled tradespeople who take pride in their craft.
