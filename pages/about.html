Our Story

Our Story

Sunshine Homes Limited was born from a simple belief: every Auckland family deserves a well-built, beautiful home delivered on time and within budget. Founded in South Auckland in 2014, we started as a small, family-owned operation and have grown into one of the region's most trusted residential construction companies.

We remain proudly family-owned and locally operated. That means every project receives the personal attention it deserves — from the first consultation right through to the handover of your keys. We're not a volume builder; we're a partner in building the place you'll call home.

Quality is never negotiated at Sunshine Homes. We work only with accredited trades, premium materials, and proven methods — because the homes we build represent not just our clients' biggest investments, but their families' futures. When you choose Sunshine Homes, you choose a team that genuinely cares about getting it right.

What We Stand For

We hold every build to the highest standard of workmanship, from foundations to finishing touches. Great materials and skilled hands make homes that last for generations.
